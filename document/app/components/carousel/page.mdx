# Carousel

Present one or more scroll-snapped slides at a time, with accessible controls, indicators, autoplay,
and custom content. Adapted from [Bootstrap v6.0.0-alpha.1 Carousel][1].

## Quick start

Wrap each slide in `CarouselSlide`. The carousel supplies previous/next controls; indicators are
optional. Use `aria-label` to name each carousel.

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Featured stories" indicators>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-4 bg-3 p-5">
                <h3>Build with Idea React</h3>
                <p className="mb-0">Slides can contain any React content.</p>
            </div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-4 bg-2 p-5">
                <h3>Compose your own content</h3>
                <p className="mb-0">Use cards, forms, images, or other components.</p>
            </div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-4 bg-3 p-5">
                <h3>Keep each slide focused</h3>
                <p className="mb-0">Give each item an appropriate accessible name.</p>
            </div>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

## Controls and indicators

`controlsPlacement` supports `overlay` (default), `top`, and `bottom`. Indicators can be shown at
the control row with `indicators`. `controls={false}` removes previous and next buttons. Overlay
controls sit on the slides; top and bottom controls reserve their own rows.

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Top controls" controlsPlacement="top" indicators>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <h3>First slide</h3>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <h3>Second slide</h3>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Bottom controls" controlsPlacement="bottom">
        <CarouselSlide>
            <p>Content can be placed above a separate control row.</p>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <p>Use the wrapper's normal children for custom layouts.</p>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Overlay controls" controlsPlacement="overlay" indicators>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-4 bg-3 p-5">Overlay slide one</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-4 bg-2 p-5">Overlay slide two</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

## Layout and appearance

Use `fade` for a crossfade and `dark` for dark-surface controls. `items`, `itemsGap`, `itemsPeek`,
and `center` configure multi-item, spaced, peeking, and centered layouts. Set `items={3}` to show
three slides, or give each slide its own width for a variable-width layout.

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Product gallery" items={3} itemsGap="1rem" itemsPeek="2rem" center>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-3 bg-3 p-4">One</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-3 bg-2 p-4">Two</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-3 bg-3 p-4">Three</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <div className="rounded-3 bg-2 p-4">Four</div>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

<CodeExample>

## Autoplay and end behavior

Autoplay is off by default. When enabled, it pauses while hovered, focused, or while the page is
hidden; manual navigation stops cycling until the play button is used again. Set a per-slide
`interval` in milliseconds to override the carousel's `interval`.

`ends` accepts `loop` (default), `wrap`, or `stop`. `stop` disables controls at the first and last
slide. `loop` continues in the same direction through a temporary, inert slide before returning
to the original content without a visible reverse jump. `wrap` jumps directly to the opposite end.
Fade, reduced motion, multi-item, peeking, centered, and variable-width layouts fall back to `wrap`.
Temporary slides are hidden from assistive technology and do not change the logical slide count.
Set `pause={false}` to keep autoplay running while
hovering or focusing the carousel.

<CodeExample>
    <Carousel aria-label="Automatic announcements" autoplay playPause indicators interval={4000}>
        <CarouselSlide interval={2000}>
            <p>Shown for two seconds.</p>
        </CarouselSlide>
        <CarouselSlide>
            <p>Shown for the default interval.</p>
        </CarouselSlide>
    </Carousel>
</CodeExample>

All variants use native scrolling and touch gestures. Keyboard users can move between slides with
the arrow keys; users of autoplay should always have the visible play/pause control.
