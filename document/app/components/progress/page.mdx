# Progress

Every progress bar needs an accessible name. `value`, `min`, and `max` determine its width and ARIA
values. Values outside the range are clamped; invalid or non-finite ranges throw a `RangeError`.

## Quick start and labels

<CodeExample>
    <div className="vstack gap-3">
        <Progress aria-label="Not started" value={0} />
        <Progress aria-label="Upload" value={25}>
            25%
        </Progress>
        <Progress aria-label="Processing" value={50} theme="info">
            50%
        </Progress>
        <Progress aria-label="Complete" value={100} theme="success">
            Complete
        </Progress>
    </div>
</CodeExample>

## Height, backgrounds, and long labels

Set height on the outer component. `barProps` forwards classes, styles, and attributes to the visual
bar, including width utilities or custom backgrounds. Prefer labels outside very short bars to avoid
clipping and contrast problems.

<CodeExample>
    <div className="vstack gap-3">
        <Progress aria-label="Thin progress" value={25} style={{ height: 1 }} />
        <Progress aria-label="Tall progress" value={75} theme="warning" style={{ height: 24 }}>
            75%
        </Progress>
        <Progress
            aria-label="Custom range"
            value={15}
            min={10}
            max={30}
            barProps={{ className: 'bg-danger' }}
        />
    </div>
</CodeExample>

## Stacked, striped, and animated

`ProgressStack` puts widths on each segment's outer element, as required by Bootstrap. Each segment
still has its own accessible name. `animated` enables stripes automatically.

<CodeExample>
    <ProgressStack>
        <Progress aria-label="Documents" value={15} theme="primary">
            15%
        </Progress>
        <Progress aria-label="Images" value={30} theme="success">
            30%
        </Progress>
        <Progress aria-label="Video" value={20} theme="info">
            20%
        </Progress>
    </ProgressStack>
    <div className="vstack gap-3 mt-3">
        <Progress aria-label="Striped progress" value={50} theme="accent" striped />
        <Progress aria-label="Active upload" value={75} theme="primary" animated />
    </div>
</CodeExample>
