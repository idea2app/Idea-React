# Nav overflow

Keep priority navigation usable in narrow containers. Items that do not fit move into a More menu;
the wrapper observes its container rather than the viewport. Adapted from
[Bootstrap v6.0.0-alpha.1 Nav overflow][1].

## Quick start

Wrap `NavLink` items in `NavOverflow`. The `variant` prop supports the standard, pill, tab, and
underline nav styles.

<CodeExample>
    <NavOverflow id="primary-overflow" variant="pills" aria-label="Primary navigation">
        <NavLink href="#overview" className="active">
            Overview
        </NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#products">Products</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#services">Services</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#reports">Reports</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#help">Help</NavLink>
    </NavOverflow>
</CodeExample>

## Tabs, underline, and priority items

Use `variant="tabs"` or `variant="underline"` for the corresponding nav style. Mark links with
`nav-overflow-keep` to keep high-priority items visible even when the rest collapse.

<CodeExample>
    <NavOverflow id="section-overflow" variant="underline" aria-label="Documentation sections">
        <NavLink href="#intro" className="nav-overflow-keep">
            Introduction
        </NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#install">Installation</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#usage">Usage</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#api">API</NavLink>
    </NavOverflow>
</CodeExample>

Disabled `NavLink` states are preserved in the overflow menu. The wrapper also accepts nested menu
components as items, so a menu remains usable when its trigger overflows.

## Navbar and menu integration

Place the wrapper inside a navbar to adapt its links to the available navbar width.

<CodeExample>
    <Navbar expand className="bg-3 px-3">
        <NavbarBrand as="span">Idea React</NavbarBrand>
        <NavOverflow id="navbar-overflow" aria-label="Site navigation">
            <NavLink href="#components">Components</NavLink>
            <NavLink href="#guides">Guides</NavLink>
            <NavLink href="#examples">Examples</NavLink>
            <NavLink href="#about">About</NavLink>
        </NavOverflow>
    </Navbar>
</CodeExample>

<CodeExample>
    <NavOverflow id="menu-overflow" variant="pills" aria-label="Product navigation">
        <NavLink href="#home">Home</NavLink>
        <Menu label="Products">
            <NavLink href="#laptops">Laptops</NavLink>
            <NavLink href="#tablets">Tablets</NavLink>
        </Menu>
        <NavLink href="#services">Services</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#support" disabled>
            Unavailable
        </NavLink>
    </NavOverflow>
</CodeExample>

## Toggle options

`moreText` changes the toggle label; set it to `false` for an icon-only toggle with an accessible
More name. `moreIcon` accepts a React node and `iconPlacement` accepts `start` or `end`.
`threshold` keeps at least that many items visible. `collapseBelow` accepts a pixel width or a
Bootstrap breakpoint such as `md` to collapse every non-priority item below that width.

<CodeExample>
    <NavOverflow
        id="custom-overflow"
        moreText="All sections"
        moreIcon={<Icon name="three-dots" aria-hidden="true" />}
        iconPlacement="end"
        threshold={2}
        collapseBelow="md"
    >
        <NavLink href="#one" className="nav-overflow-keep">
            One
        </NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#two">Two</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#three">Three</NavLink>
        <NavLink href="#four">Four</NavLink>
    </NavOverflow>
</CodeExample>

Use `menuPlacement` and `menuStrategy="fixed"` when an ancestor clips the menu. Call
`updateOverflow()` after changing external layout constraints; resize observation runs automatically.
