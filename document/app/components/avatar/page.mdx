# Avatar

Avatars represent users or entities with an image or initials as a fallback. The Idea React
[`Avatar`][1] wrapper follows the [Bootstrap 6 avatar guide][2] with `.avatar`, `.avatar-img`,
status indicators, and stacks.

## Image

<Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" />

Pass `src` to render an `.avatar-img` image inside the `.avatar` wrapper.

## Initials

<div className="d-flex flex-wrap gap-2">
    <Avatar>AB</Avatar>
    <Avatar theme="primary">CD</Avatar>
    <Avatar theme="success">EF</Avatar>
    <Avatar subtle theme="primary">
        GH
    </Avatar>
    <Avatar subtle theme="danger">
        IJ
    </Avatar>
</div>

Text children render as initials. `theme` selects the semantic color and `subtle` switches to the
subtle variant.

`size` accepts the official `xs`, `sm`, `lg`, and `xl` classes; a number sets a custom
`--bs-avatar-size` in `rem`.

## Status indicator

<div className="d-flex flex-wrap gap-2">
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" status="online" />
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" status="offline" />
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" status="busy" />
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" status="away" />
</div>

`status` renders an `.avatar-status` indicator with `role="img"` and an accessible label, since the
status is conveyed purely through color and shape. Override the announcement with `statusLabel`.

## Avatar stack

<AvatarStack size="lg">
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/idea2app.png" alt="idea2app" />
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/mdo.png" alt="mdo" />
    <Avatar src="https://github.com/twbs.png" alt="twbs" />

`AvatarStack` groups avatars with an overlapping effect; its `size` applies to the whole stack.
