# Dialogs

Transform dialogs to serve many purposes, from simple confirmations to focused feature
introductions. The examples below use the documentation site's reusable interactive demo.

## Confirmation

Keep the requested action and its consequence clear.

<DialogDemo label="Delete item" title="Delete this item?" saveLabel="Delete">
    <p>This action cannot be undone. The item will be permanently removed.</p>
</DialogDemo>

## Feature introduction

Use a larger body to explain a new capability, and offer a next step.

<DialogDemo label="Explore updates" title="A faster way to collaborate" saveLabel="Take a tour">
    <p>
        Share work with your team, collect feedback, and keep every decision close to the project.
    </p>
</DialogDemo>

## Sign-up dialog

Ask for the minimum information needed to continue, and keep the form inside the dialog.

<DialogDemo label="Create account" title="Sign up for free" saveLabel="Create account">
    <FormLabel htmlFor="dialog-email">Email address</FormLabel>
    <FormControl id="dialog-email" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" />
</DialogDemo>
