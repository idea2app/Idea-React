# List groups

Extend list groups with custom content to present notifications, navigation, and other collections.

## Notification list

Combine a strong title with supporting text and a status badge.

<CodeExample>
    <ListGroup>
        {inbox.map(({ title, detail, count }, index) => (
            <ListGroupItem key={title} as="a" href={`#message-${index}`} action>
                <div className="d-flex justify-content-between gap-3">
                    <strong>{title}</strong>
                    {count && <Badge theme="primary">{count}</Badge>}
                </div>
                <span className="d-block small fg-2 mt-1">{detail}</span>
            </ListGroupItem>
        ))}
    </ListGroup>
</CodeExample>

## Numbered steps

Use an ordered list when the sequence of items matters.

<CodeExample>
    <ListGroup as="ol" numbered>
        <ListGroupItem>Choose a plan</ListGroupItem>
        <ListGroupItem>Invite your team</ListGroupItem>
        <ListGroupItem>Launch your workspace</ListGroupItem>
    </ListGroup>
</CodeExample>

## Horizontal list

For short labels, switch to a horizontal list on wider screens.

<CodeExample>
    <ListGroup horizontal="md">
        <ListGroupItem>All</ListGroupItem>
        <ListGroupItem active>Active</ListGroupItem>
        <ListGroupItem>Archived</ListGroupItem>
    </ListGroup>
</CodeExample>
