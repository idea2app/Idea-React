# Jumbotrons

Create a prominent introductory section with a headline, explanatory copy, and a call to action.
Use Bootstrap utilities to shape the content without requiring a separate component.

## Simple jumbotron

Give the message room to breathe with a contrasting surface and generous spacing.

<CodeExample>
    <Container className="rounded-5 bg-1 p-7 lg:p-9">
        <h2 className="display-5 fw-semibold">A flexible workspace for every team</h2>
        <p className="text-lg fg-2 max-w-3xl">
            Bring projects, people, and progress together in one place.
        </p>
        <Button variant="solid" theme="primary" size="lg">
            Explore the product
        </Button>
    </Container>
</CodeExample>

## Full-width introduction

Use a full-width background to distinguish a page introduction from the content that follows.

<CodeExample>
    <section className="bg-dark py-9" data-bs-theme="dark">
        <Container>
            <p className="text-uppercase fw-semibold">Made for teams</p>
            <h2 className="display-4 fw-semibold">Make room for better ideas.</h2>
            <p className="text-lg max-w-3xl">
                Start with a clear plan, bring everyone into the conversation, and deliver work
                you're proud of.
            </p>
            <Button variant="solid" theme="light">
                Start a project
            </Button>
        </Container>
    </section>
</CodeExample>
