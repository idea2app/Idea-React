# Heroes

Set the stage on a homepage with a concise headline, supporting text, and a clear call to action.

## Centered hero

Use a focused message when one action is the main goal of the page.

<CodeExample>
    <Container className="py-12 text-center">
        <h2 className="display-5 fw-semibold text-balance">Build something remarkable</h2>
        <p className="text-lg fg-2 mx-auto max-w-3xl">
            Bring your next idea to life with flexible tools that grow with your team.
        </p>
        <Button variant="solid" theme="primary" size="lg">
            Get started
        </Button>
    </Container>
</CodeExample>

## Split hero

Place the message and its actions beside a supporting visual or product preview.

<CodeExample>
    <div className="d-flex flex-column md:flex-row align-items-center gap-7 rounded-5 bg-1 p-7">
        <div className="flex-fill">
            <p className="text-uppercase fw-semibold fg-accent">Introducing Idea</p>
            <h2 className="display-6 fw-semibold">A clearer way to build</h2>
            <p className="fg-2">
                Keep your interface consistent from the first screen to the last.
            </p>
            <div className="d-flex flex-wrap gap-3">
                <Button variant="solid" theme="primary">
                    Explore the product
                </Button>
                <Button variant="outline" theme="secondary">
                    Learn more
                </Button>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div className="flex-fill rounded-4 bg-body p-7 text-center">
            <span className="display-4 fw-semibold">Idea React</span>
        </div>
    </div>
</CodeExample>

## Hero with product preview

Use a contained preview to balance the headline without competing with its call to action.

<CodeExample>
    <Container className="d-flex flex-column align-items-center gap-5 py-9 text-center">
        <h2 className="display-5 fw-semibold max-w-4xl">
            Documentation that keeps your team moving
        </h2>
        <p className="text-lg fg-2 max-w-3xl">
            Find the patterns you need, understand how they work, and get back to building.
        </p>
        <div className="d-flex flex-wrap gap-3 justify-content-center">
            <Button variant="solid" theme="primary">
                Read the docs
            </Button>
            <Button variant="outline" theme="secondary">
                View the components
            </Button>
        </div>
        <div className="w-100 rounded-5 bg-1 p-8">
            <span className="display-6 fw-semibold">Idea React</span>
        </div>
    </Container>
</CodeExample>

## Sign-up hero

Keep the form short and make its purpose clear before asking visitors to submit it.

<CodeExample>
    <div className="d-flex flex-column lg:flex-row align-items-center gap-7 rounded-5 bg-1 p-7">
        <div className="flex-fill">
            <h2 className="display-6 fw-semibold">Start building today</h2>
            <p className="fg-2">Create a workspace for your next project.</p>
        </div>
        <form className="d-flex flex-column gap-3 w-100 max-w-3xl">
            <label htmlFor="hero-email">Email address</label>
            <FormControl id="hero-email" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" />
            <Button type="submit" variant="solid" theme="primary">
                Sign up
            </Button>
        </form>
    </div>
</CodeExample>

## Dark hero

Use contrast to make a focused call to action stand out from surrounding sections.

<CodeExample>
    <section className="rounded-5 bg-dark p-7 lg:p-9" data-bs-theme="dark">

        <p className="text-lg fg-2 max-w-3xl">
            Bring the people and ideas behind your work into one shared space.
        </p>
        <Button variant="solid" theme="light">
            Join the team
        </Button>
    </section>
</CodeExample>
