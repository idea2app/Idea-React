# Form controls

Styled native inputs, textareas and selects preserve browser validation and form submission.

## Quick start

<CodeExample>
    <FormField>
        <FormLabel htmlFor="controlEmail">Email</FormLabel>
        <FormControl id="controlEmail" type="email" placeholder="name@example.com" required />
        <FormText>Choose `controlSize="sm"` or `"lg"` for compact or large fields.</FormText>
    </FormField>
    <FormTextArea aria-label="Message" rows={3} controlSize="lg" className="mt-3" />
    <FormSelect aria-label="Country" controlSize="sm" defaultValue="ca" className="mt-3">
        <option value="ca">Canada</option>
        <option value="us">United States</option>
    </FormSelect>
    <FormControl aria-label="Read only" plaintext readOnly value="Readonly text" className="mt-3" />
    <FormControl aria-label="Color" type="color" color defaultValue="#663399" className="mt-3" />
    <FormControl aria-label="Files" type="file" multiple controlSize="sm" className="mt-3" />
    <FormControl
        aria-label="City"
        list="controlCities"
        placeholder="Choose or type a city"
        className="mt-3"
    />
    <datalist id="controlCities">
        <option value="Paris" />
        <option value="Tokyo" />
    </datalist>
    <FormControl aria-label="Date and time" type="datetime-local" caret className="mt-3" />
    <FormGhost aria-label="Borderless search" placeholder="Ghost input" className="mt-3" />
</CodeExample>

Use native `type` for email, password, number, search, date, time, month and week.
`caret` adds a picker affordance; `color` styles color inputs. `disabled`, `readOnly`, `multiple`,
`size`, `min`, `max`, `step` and `list` retain their native meanings. `FormSelect multiple`
accepts array values. `validation="valid"` or `"invalid"` sets visual and accessible state;
associate `FormFeedback` and `FormText` using `aria-describedby`.
